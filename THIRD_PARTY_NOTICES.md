# Third-party assets

The original Rook Lite logo (`public/images/rook-logo.png` and its WebP derivative) and the application interface shown in product screenshots come from [volkanto/rook-lite](https://github.com/volkanto/rook-lite), version 1.6.0, commit `19834729d368fa3a5aca04345fd28101b79d0790`.

Rook Lite is licensed under Apache 2.0. The license is reproduced in `docs/licenses/rook-lite.txt`. The WebP logo is resized; screenshots are captured from the unmodified application, resized to WebP, and visually cropped or framed by the website. All note content is synthetic demo content. The site's social cards combine the same real interface with original marketing typography.
